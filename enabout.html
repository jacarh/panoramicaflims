REAL MOMENTS, WITHIN TIMELESS STORIES

Alejandro

Cinematography with an intentional and precise eye. He is interested in composition, light, and the way an image can hold an emotion without needing to explain it. His way of working is detailed and conscious, taking care of every element within the frame, yet always leaving room for the spontaneous moments that occur in front of the camera—those that cannot be fully planned.

Camila

Focuses on the human element behind the image. She has a special sensitivity for the voice, the silences, and the real gestures that appear effortlessly. Her approach is calm and observant, with the intention of accompanying what happens without intervening, seeking stories that feel honest, close, and faithful to the people who live them.

WHY PANORÁMICA?

We work in perfect synchronicity, fusing photography and film into a cohesive and complete narrative of your big day.

The construction of every story stems from a balance between the image and the human element. We are interested in the relationship between what is seen and what is felt, between composition and spontaneity, between aesthetics and truth.

We move between visual intent and narrative sensitivity, observing how people express themselves naturally in front of the camera. Our intent is for every story to preserve its authenticity, without intervening more than necessary, allowing what is real to have its space within a carefully crafted structure.
